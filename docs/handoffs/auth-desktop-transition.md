# Desktop auth transition

- `DesktopAuthTransition` wraps the auth stack and keeps the campus image and login polygon mounted between welcome/login routes at ≥900px. Its Animated value moves the scene left over 680ms while fading it slightly; the persistent login panel slides in. The navigator theme/background is transparent within this auth layout so the shared scene remains visible.
- `useDesktopAuthPageMotion` fades/slides departing content for 140ms, replaces the route after 150ms, and enters new content over 480ms. Login's back button reverses the flow. Direct login and reduced-motion navigation render their final positions immediately.
- Duplicate transitions are blocked. Timers/animations are cancelled on unmount, unrelated navigation or responsive deactivation. Unexpected history navigation resets the scene to the actual route. OS/browser reduced-motion preferences are observed.
- `AuthScreen` gives desktop `/login` and `/register` the same right-hand form layout, full-size brand and back control. Both forms use the persistent campus, notched border and forward/reverse welcome transition. The longer registration form scrolls within its side. Existing form fields, Google actions, validation, auth APIs and mobile layouts are reused.
- Final login framing gives the illustration the left 50–65% of the canvas and places the form in the rightmost 36%. The illustration settles at the left canvas edge, preserving the student group on wide screens. Its right boundary repeats the welcome screen's diagonal/notch geometry and translucent band.
- TypeScript, the 8-size welcome browser regression and `auth-desktop-transition-browser-check.cjs` pass. Checks cover forward/reverse movement, 1024/1366/1440/1920px login layouts, direct entry, reduced motion, required-field errors, mobile fallback and one successful mocked login. Real browser visuals and image loading were inspected; screenshots are ignored under `dist/auth-transition-verification/`.
- No dependency or backend changes. Physical native-device transition behavior was not tested.
