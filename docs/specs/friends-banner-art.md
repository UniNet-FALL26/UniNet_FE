The LEFT 53% must remain quiet almost empty pale icy blue (#EDF5FF), with only extremely faint campus silhouettes along bottom edge, suitable for live dark HTML text over it. On RIGHT 47%, reproduce the friendly clean 2D editorial cartoon illustration style in the reference, NOT 3D, NOT anime, NOT photo: three smiling Vietnamese university students, waist-up, bottoms cropped flush at bottom edge. Male student left blue hoodie and navy backpack, young woman center white blouse holding a blue-grey tablet/book, male right white shirt round glasses headphones around neck navy backpack. Heads fit fully within the frame with gentle top padding. Behind them very pale blue campus buildings and trees and subtle clouds, a few small chat bubbles blue/white with three dots. Cohesive gentle pastel blues, navy outlines, warm skin tones, polished soft shading. Characters clustered closely in the right half and blend naturally into the background without a rectangular image boundary. EXACT intended composition of the supplied reference, plain empty left space for separately rendered UI text. No border no rounded container no shadow; full-bleed rectangular art.

## Integration

The generated bitmap is 2079x756. Contain and position it at the banner's right
edge with its pale background blending into the container. Desktop shows text
at left and students at right. Mobile moves the illustration below the copy.
